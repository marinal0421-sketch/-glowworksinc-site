About

A production company for large-scale light experiences.

Glow Works Inc. is a California corporation specializing in large-scale, immersive light show experiences. West Harbor is our first production — and the starting point for expansion across California, then nationwide.

The company is founder-led and based in the Los Angeles area.

Investor Relations

A flagship built to scale.

Harbor Glow at West Harbor is our first production. We’re using it to prove the format — audience demand, venue economics, and the production playbook — before expanding across California, then nationwide.

Our approach is asset-light: Glow Works designs, imports, and produces the lighting experience; venue partners provide the site and its existing foot traffic.
